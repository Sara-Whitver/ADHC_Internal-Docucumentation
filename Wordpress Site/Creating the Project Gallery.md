# How To Configure And Retrieve YMC Filter Shortcodes

Learn how to navigate the YMC Filter settings within your WordPress dashboard to customize post and filter layouts. This guide also explains how to locate and copy the necessary shortcodes to integrate your configured grids seamlessly.

1\. Navigate to the Wordpress admin dashboard

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/d8245285-9c44-49b2-b99e-576c410baf05/action-89451b47818349ab9f2dee183554e167_579809f4d0bb4453868d9bb2e88544cb_text_export.jpeg)


#### Open Filter Manager


2\. Click "YMC Filter"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/d8245285-9c44-49b2-b99e-576c410baf05/action-89451b47818349ab9f2dee183554e167_6e3a2a777f2449bcb8f2b65966770d83_text_export.jpeg)


3\. Click "Add Filter".

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/7a23a0a8-7368-4af9-b55a-3beb522a3451/action-e70d645b3a0c44babae7c43ac4387384_79d59e2b38804ef398e554cb0938f67f_text_export.jpeg)


4\. Expand the "Query Params" menu

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/2a62a624-a9f7-4b35-9b59-cc46767293bd/action-f6010c81bd2e4c0f95c96f2ddc6412e1_b563ac924b4d40729969a85d0d4b1099_text_export.jpeg)

7\. Click "Layouts"
