# How To Create A New Taxonomy In WordPress Using ACF
#### [Made by Alabama Digital Humanities Center with Scribe](https://scribehow.com/o/Q5Aaj_WQQLSLiZKvgOpJpw/viewer/How_To_Create_A_New_Taxonomy_In_WordPress_Using_ACF__iRxL615-SWGlTx9QelMj0g)
Learn how to efficiently manage custom content categorization by adding a new taxonomy to your WordPress site. This guide walks you through the Advanced Custom Fields settings to help you organize your posts effectively.

1\. Navigate to the Wordpress admin dashboard

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/6b904e83-a082-41d2-8057-b98ac057ef30/action-cd264af048fc4fc8833015b8fbcc427f_56e2a1550ee34b1b8337be2b423ce711_text_export.jpeg)


#### Open Taxonomy Settings


2\. Click "ACF"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/6b904e83-a082-41d2-8057-b98ac057ef30/action-cd264af048fc4fc8833015b8fbcc427f_03b9d1747b7d4955b80b8095f376db38_text_export.jpeg)


3\. Click "Taxonomies"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/800a19ba-4c9a-4b0b-b21d-6d9030fc52d1/action-a6fe00345d294f0ba4555966851a8dde_16205749c32c409fa0b9bc050f0c9753_text_export.jpeg)


4\. Click "Add New"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/356de9d6-30ab-470a-b929-7d4e45ab9859/action-fa7cb8c368594fcc98dfc6ee5f93d67f_a740c896799642ea9bf9e16e2ab87b80_text_export.jpeg)


#### Configure Taxonomy


5\. Enter the plural label, singular label, and taxonomy key.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/28a45336-1d94-487b-9edf-d8832ab50f3b/action-4a1bbe34cd89424cb182910d08d9716d_6d637a7bb16547f88a7082495ba3e672_text_export.jpeg)


6\. Select Post as the post type.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/872d1759-0c59-4fbd-ace3-a8eca37d2ffe/action-ab3010484cde49d698982e55ded60567_6f1f867f543e42a8b7a98148e9068afa_text_export.jpeg)

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files/2026-10-28/db9c32d9-1aa9-4fbd-92c9-ead54fe32894/action-0e34be72701c4ba8aa33bd01d5516951_b06272a8792649a69d046b859e37ee98_text_export.jpeg)


#### Save Taxonomy


7\. Click "Save Changes"
